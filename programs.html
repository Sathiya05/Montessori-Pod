Thoughtfully designed Montessori programs that meet children where they are—nurturing independence, curiosity, and joyful learning at every stage.

18 months – 3 years

Toddler Program

A nurturing first classroom experience where toddlers build trust, independence, and a sense of belonging through gentle guidance and purposeful activity.

  • Early independence and self-care
  • Language development through songs and stories
  • Sensory exploration with natural materials
  • Practical life activities
  • Social interaction in a caring community

3 – 6 years

Primary Program

A vibrant prepared environment where children explore foundational concepts through hands-on materials, building concentration, confidence, and a deep love of learning.

  • Practical life and coordination
  • Sensorial learning and refinement
  • Language and early literacy
  • Mathematics with concrete materials
  • Cultural studies: science, geography, and arts
Learn More
5 – 6 years

Kindergarten Program

A purposeful bridge to future schooling, where children strengthen academic foundations, social skills, and confidence as capable, independent learners.

  • School readiness and routines
  • Early literacy and writing
  • Mathematical reasoning
  • Collaboration and leadership
  • Independent learning and problem solving
