Foundation

Practical Life

Practical life activities help children develop coordination, concentration, independence, and care for themselves and their environment. These meaningful tasks build the foundation for all later learning.

  • Pouring and transferring
  • Sorting and organizing
  • Cleaning and care of environment
  • Dressing frames and self-care
  • Everyday independence and grace & courtesy

Exploration

Sensorial Learning

Sensorial materials refine the child's senses and help them classify, compare, and make sense of the world. Through touch, sight, sound, and movement, children build the cognitive foundations for math, language, and science.

  • Shape and color recognition
  • Texture exploration and tactile discrimination
  • Sound discrimination and auditory awareness
  • Size, dimension, weight, and volume
  • Classic sensorial materials like the Pink Tower and Color Tablets

Communication

Language and Literacy

Language development is woven throughout the Montessori day. From rich conversation and storytelling to phonetic materials, children build strong foundations for reading, writing, and confident expression.

  • Vocabulary building through conversation and naming
  • Storytelling, songs, and read-alouds
  • Phonics and sound-letter relationships
  • Reading readiness with sandpaper letters and movable alphabet
  • Early writing through fine motor and language work

Discovery Areas

Mathematics and Cultural Studies

Mathematics

Children move from concrete to abstract understanding using beautiful, tactile materials that make mathematical ideas visible and engaging.

  • Number recognition
  • Counting and quantity
  • Patterns and sequencing
  • Quantity relationships
  • Hands-on mathematical materials (beads, rods, number cards)

Cultural Studies

Cultural lessons inspire wonder about the world, connecting children to nature, places, people, and the interdependence of life.

  • Nature and botany
  • Geography and the world
  • Science exploration
  • Community and belonging
  • Cultural awareness and celebrations
